▤ Simple Touch Experimental · v0.1.0

A small bridge.
A more comfortable home.

Turn your Simple Touch shades into local Home Assistant controls. No cloud account. No configuration files.

01 · Connect your bridge

Start with a USB cable.

Connect a Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 with its wired CC1101 radio. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer and close other serial monitors.

Open this page in a browser with Web Serial support, such as desktop Chrome or Edge.Use the HTTPS installer or serve this folder on localhost.

The firmware package is not present yet. Download the complete installer artifact from the repository’s Actions page.

Already installed? Connect to change Wi-Fi or visit your bridge. Export a remote backup before erasing or replacing an existing bridge.

02 · Make it yours

Connect Wi-Fi. Add a shade.

The installer helps you choose a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Select Visit device, then Add shade. The bridge walks you through pairing with your existing remote.

03 · Bring it home

Add Home Assistant.

Install the integration through HACS, restart Home Assistant, then add Simple Touch under Devices & services. Your paired shades appear automatically.
